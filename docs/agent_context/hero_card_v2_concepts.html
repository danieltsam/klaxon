Hero Alarm Card — 5 New Aesthetic Directions

Random explorations for the next iteration of the home-screen hero card. Each concept is a distinct aesthetic direction with its own animation personality. Live CSS mockups below.

Concept α • Ambient / Cinematic
Constellation Drift
Each enabled alarm is a floating star. The hero is the brightest, gently pulsing in soft purple. Toggling feels like igniting or extinguishing a star. The whole card becomes a quiet, atmospheric status surface.

Concept γ • Heartbeat / Alive
Glassmorphic Pulse
Frosted glass card with a soft breathing border. The pulse rate is proportional to time-to-fire — slow and meditative when far away, faster and urgent as the alarm approaches. Card refracts the alarm list behind it.

Concept δ • Spatial / Systems
Orbital Lattice
Three concentric dashed orbits around the hero time. Each enabled alarm is a satellite — closer orbits are more recent, distant ones are further out. Satellites move slowly along their orbits, communicating "this alarm is armed and scheduled."

Concept ε • Editorial / Print
Editorial Marquee
Editorial newspaper feel — huge serif time at the center, scrolling marquee ticker at the bottom listing all enabled alarms. Blinking colon gives mechanical precision. Two thin horizontal rules frame the time like a printed headline.

About these concepts. These are exploratory aesthetic directions for the next hero-card iteration, distinct from the A–E backlog entries.
